# T41-R0B notes (Teensy 4.1 adapter)

R0B keeps the R0A schematic, placement and routing except around the USB-C connector. A design review on 2026-11-19 found three problems that would most likely stop USB from working, or overheat the supply path. R0B fixes all three.

## Changes

- **Keep the Teensy VIN–VUSB pads joined.** R0A said to cut them. Only D+ and D− reach the Teensy through the pogo pins, so with the pads cut nothing powers VUSB. The i.MX RT1062 USB PHY is supplied from VBUS on VUSB, so the Teensy would not enumerate. With the pads joined, carrier VBUS → F1 → +5 V → D2 → VIN also powers VUSB. **Never plug the Teensy's own micro-USB** while it sits on the carrier, because the carrier would then drive 5 V back into that host port. The silkscreen now reads *KEEP VIN-VUSB JOINED*.
- **USB D+/D− have no stubs.** R0A continued each data line about 90 mm across the board on inner layers to test pads TP7/TP8, and the USBLC6 (U1) sat on a 10 mm side branch. R0B removes TP7/TP8 and places U1 directly behind the pogo pins with flow-through pinning (pin 4 → 3 and 6 → 1). Each line is now about 14 mm long, entirely on F.Cu, with no vias.
- **VBUS is a wide outer-layer path.** R0A fed F1 through about 20 mm of 0.2 mm inner-layer track and one via, plus a 100 mm branch to TP4 that cut through the +5 V pour. R0B removes TP4 and its branch, and routes J1 → F1 as a 1.0 mm B.Cu track with four 0.3 mm vias at the connector and three at the fuse.

Test pads TP4, TP7 and TP8 are gone; the remaining pads keep their numbers. The layout change was applied to the routed R0A board with [rework_r0b.py](../hardware/teensy41/tools/rework_r0b.py), and [design.py](../hardware/teensy41/tools/design.py) carries the new U1 position and test-pad list.

## Checks

KiCad 10.0.8 reports zero ERC findings and zero DRC, unconnected-item and schematic-parity findings at all severities ([reports](../tests/T41-R0B/)). The R0B Gerber package was exported from the R0B board; it is now superseded by the [T41-R0C package](../manufacturing/prototype/T41-R0C/README.md) and remains in the git history. The previous R0A package did not match the committed R0A board (602 against 614 drill hits), so do not use the R0A Gerbers.

The ADBMS6822 configuration was checked against the ADBMS6821/ADBMS6822 datasheet (Rev. B):

| Pin | Connection | Result |
|---|---|---|
| PHAPOL | GND | SPI mode 0 (CPOL = 0, CPHA = 0), matches the firmware |
| XCVRMD | GND | Standard bidirectional mode |
| RTO | GND | 1.5 s low-power timeout |
| VDD / VP | +5 V | Inside the 3.0–5.5 V VDD range; VP tied to VDD as the datasheet allows |
| VDDS | +3.3 V | Inside the 1.7–5.5 V range |
| INTR / WAKE | Teensy inputs | Weak (about 20 µA) pull-ups to VDD; INTR only toggles in timeout-monitor mode, which XCVRMD = GND does not select |
| Centre taps | Open | The transmitter does not need them |

## Still open

- **ADBMS6822 decoupling.** The datasheet asks for at least 1 µF as close as possible to VDD. Each bridge has two 1 µF and four 100 nF capacitors 6–11 mm from its pins. Every VDD/VP pin has a via about 2.5 mm away into the +5 V pour between ground planes, which limits the loop, but a layout revision should put a 100 nF capacitor at each VDD, VP and VDDS pin. The ISO1042 VCC2 and MCP2518FD VDD capacitors are about 5 mm away.
- **Pogo pins.** J4/J5 are 12 mm tall on a 2 mm SMD pad and can tilt during reflow. Either check them in JLCPCB's assembly preview and inspect after reflow, or remove J4/J5 from the BOM and CPL and solder them by hand with the Teensy in place as an alignment jig.
- **Insulation class.** SM91801AL-E gives basic insulation, and the USB side is touchable through the host PC. A 1,000 V pack normally needs reinforced insulation to touchable circuits (about 10 mm creepage instead of 8 mm), or a second barrier such as a USB isolator. See [decisions](decisions.md#design-review-2026-11-19).
- **Hot-plug capacitance.** About 13–17 µF sits on +5 V, above the 10 µF USB limit. Measure the inrush on the bench.
- **Net classes.** `design.py` defines wider VBUS and supply classes, but they do not reach the KiCad project, so a fresh `route` run uses 0.2 mm tracks for VBUS again.
