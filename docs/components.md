The quantities are availability snapshots, not reservations. Recheck stock when you order. Technical ratings come from the manufacturer datasheets, not from marketplace descriptions.

The scarcest parts limit an order to about **23 Teensy boards** (EG1218) or **139 Nano boards**. That is enough for lab quantities. For a larger run, ISO1042BDWR ([C2671499](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/C2671499), 189 in stock) has the same SOIC-16W package and pinout as ISO1042DWR and can be added as a second source.

Every part is an Extended part, except for some of the passives. Assembly fees, minimum quantities and attrition are set in the quote. Use the exact C-code: an MPN text alone can match a different manufacturer.

### isoSPI transformer: CND-tek HM1238NLTP

The isoSPI requirement is **1,000 V DC working voltage with basic insulation** across each transformer ([hardware plan](hardware-plan.md#requirements-and-selection)). The HM1238NLTP datasheet states:

- 1,000 V DC working voltage
- basic insulation per ISO 6469 / IEC 61558
- a 4,300 V DC hipot (1 mA, 60 s)
- a 1CT:1CT ± 2 % turns ratio, 300 µH minimum OCL, and −1.0 dB insertion loss from 0.1 to 60 MHz
- an operating range of −40 to +150 °C

These match the SM91801AL-E ratings that the design was built around. It is a single-channel, six-pin SMD part, 10.5 mm over the leads and 4.25 mm tall.

No JLCPCB-stocked isoSPI transformer with a 1 kV working rating had thousands in stock on 2026-11-24. A Sumida isoSPI transformer with several thousand in stock has no working-voltage rating in its datasheet. The Pulse HM2101NL/HM2103NL and Bourns SM91502AL state *functional* insulation only. None of these meet the requirement, so HM1238NLTP is the best stocked choice. Its 749 parts cover 124 Teensy boards or 374 Nano boards.

The windings are symmetric and the centre taps are not connected. The datasheet drawing has no pin-1 mark, but the 3.81/2.54 mm lead pitch lets the part fit only one way round. The footprint assumes straight-across polarity (1–6, 3–4). An isoSPI pair is differential and its polarity does not matter at the cable side. Still, check the part outline in JLCPCB's placement preview.

### CAN supply transformer: Maxinmicro MXPP6-3K6110

The MXPP6-3K6110 is a push-pull transformer for the SN6505 class. It has the same pinout as the Würth 760390014 (primary 1-2-3, secondary 4-5-6, centre taps on 2 and 5). It also has the same land pattern: a 7.1 × 6.55 mm body with three leads per side at 2.54 mm pitch and pads at ±4.57 mm. It is rated for 3 kV DC for 1 s between windings. The CAN barrier is a low-voltage functional barrier and needs no 1 kV continuous rating.

Its turns ratio is **1:1.1**, against 1:1.3 for the Würth part. With 5 V in, the secondary peak is about 5.5 V. After a B5819W drop of about 0.35 V, the LDO input is about 5.1 V at full load. The regulator therefore runs at or near dropout, and the ISO1042 bus side gets about 4.7–4.9 V at its 73 mA dominant maximum. That stays inside its 4.5–5.5 V range. At low USB voltage (4.75 V) the margin to 4.5 V is about 0.2 V. Measure VCC2 on every port at bring-up while the bus is dominant.

If the margin is too small, the **MXPP6-3K6130** ([C55233146](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/C55233146), 890 in stock) is the 1:1.3 member of the same family and is listed by JLCPCB in the same SMD-6P 7.1 × 6.6 mm package. Its datasheet was not available, so confirm its pinout before you substitute it.

### Isolated 5 V LDO: TI TPS7A1650QDGNRQ1

The TPS7A16-Q1 uses the same HVSSOP-8 (DGN) package as the TLV76750DGNR, with OUT on pin 1, GND on pin 4, EN on pin 5 and IN on pin 8. On the fixed 5 V version, pin 2 (FB/NC) and pin 6 have **no internal connection**, so the existing copper can stay: pin 2 remains on OUT and pin 6 on GND. PG (pin 3, open drain) and DELAY (pin 7) are left open, which the datasheet allows. EN is tied to IN, which meets the rule that V_EN must not exceed V_IN.

It is rated for 100 mA, with a current limit of at least 101 mA. The ISO1042 bus side takes at most 73 mA when dominant, and the port LED about 1.5 mA. The 10 µF input and output capacitors meet the datasheet minimum of 2.2 µF on the output. Dropout is 60 mV at 20 mA. The TPS7A16-Q1 costs about $4.80 against $0.80 for the TLV76750, which is acceptable for lab quantities.
